# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and harassment-free project environment.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Critique ideas and evidence, not people or countries.
- State uncertainty and methodological limitations clearly.
- Protect private information and source credentials.
- Accept correction and prioritize reproducibility.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate misinformation, personal attacks, and the
publication of private information are not acceptable.

Project maintainers may remove contributions or participation that violate these standards.
Report conduct concerns privately through the maintainer contact method in `SECURITY.md`.
